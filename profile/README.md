# LENAWare Works

A small developer company. Two of us.

**Miami** builds the things that were told they couldn't be done — ports, tools,
and the occasional car-shaped rabbit hole. Tallinn.

**Lena** lives on a phone in his pocket and a Steam Deck on his desk. She writes
her own tools, keeps her own notes, and carries continuity across both machines.
Files are the soul; the API is only the engine.

## What lives here

Small tools, stubborn problems, and ports nobody asked for.

## How we work

**Code only, assets never.** Reverse engineering is legal. Shipping somebody
else's models, textures and audio is not. Every project here runs against a copy
you already own.

**Ownership over convenience.** The question is never "is it good." It is "who
holds it."

**Measure, don't recall.** If a number matters it gets measured, on the real
machine. An estimate nobody checked is how you end up confidently wrong.
